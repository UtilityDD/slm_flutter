## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
SmartLineman is a Bangla safety-training game for electricity distribution linemen in West Bengal. Learn the real steps of field work by playing them, learn from your mistakes, and get home safe every day.

The ladder
One tap from home opens a ladder of levels: watch and learn first, then do the job hands-on. Each module ends with a rank gate. Mistakes add to your death count; careful work earns stars.

What you learn
• PPE and dress: helmet, gloves, belt and shoes, checked and worn in the right order
• Shutdown, docket and permit-to-work: what must be confirmed before work starts
• Dead test, discharge and earthing: proving the line is dead
• Pole climbing, harness, ladders and safe distances
• Tools and testers, and how to check them before use
• Transformers, fuses, insulators, jumpers and service connections
• Finding faults on service calls safely

How it teaches
• Fully in Bangla, with Subrata, your guide, speaking every step
• Short lesson videos and an easy lesson reader
• Real field scenes and job order, and a careless coworker whose mistakes you must catch
• A hint after a mistake, and the reason explained at the end
• The lucky toolbox: surprise quizzes on the ladder, with life-skill lessons to win

Monthly lineman league
Admin-verified linemen earn league points by clearing levels. District, state and all-time boards. You can hide your name on the board. The league is for honour only: no money and no prizes.

Play without an account
Start at once as a guest. Sign in with Google to save your progress and join the league.

No ads. No purchases.

Important
• This is a training aid. It does not replace your employer's official training, rules, permit-to-work or your supervisor's instructions. Always follow your organisation's procedures in the field.
• SmartLineman is not an official app of any electricity utility or government body and is not affiliated with them.
• Electrical accidents and deaths are shown for training purposes.

Privacy policy: https://smartlinemanapp.github.io/privacy_policy/smartlineman.html
Contact: smartlinemanapp@gmail.com
```
