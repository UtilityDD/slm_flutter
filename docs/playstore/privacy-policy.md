ফোনে কিছু তথ্য রাখা হয় (খেলার সেভ, ডাউনলোড করা কুইজ ও পাঠ, শেষ দেখা লিগ বোর্ড) যাতে ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও অ্যাপ চলে।

## English

### 1. Who we are

SmartLineman (package `com.smartlineman.lineman`) is a Bangla safety-training game for electricity distribution linemen in West Bengal, India. It is run by **[OWNER_NAME]** (individual developer). Contact: **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**.

The app is not an official app of any electricity utility or government body and is not affiliated with them. The same account also works on the website [smartlineman.in](https://smartlineman.in); this policy covers account data in both places.

### 2. What we collect and why

**If you play as a Guest**, no account is created. Your progress, points and settings stay only on your phone and are not sent to our servers.

**If you sign in with Google**, we store:

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, profile photo link | Your Google account (at sign-in) | Identify your account, show your profile |
| Account ID (a code) | Our sign-in system | Link your data to your account |
| Phone number (optional) | You type it | Admin verification, contact if needed |
| District, block, job type | You choose them | Verification, district league board |
| SLM ID, role, verification and block status | The app and admins | Know who is verified, prevent abuse |
| Points, finished levels and lessons, level stars, quiz answers | While you play | Save progress, scoring, the league |
| Whether to show your name in the league | Your setting | Let you hide your name on the board |

We do **not** collect: your location (GPS), contacts, photos or files, microphone, camera, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no purchases. Read-aloud voices are made by your phone's own text-to-speech.

Some data is kept on your phone (game save, downloaded quizzes and lessons, the last league board) so the app works offline.

### 3. Google sign-in

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In. Google gives us your name, email and profile photo link. We never see your Google password. How Google handles data: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Where data is stored (Supabase)

Account and game data are stored with **Supabase**, a cloud database service that processes data on our behalf (data processor). Server region: **[SUPABASE_REGION]**. Supabase's policy: [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy). Large images, audio and lessons download from our website smartlineman.in; like any server it may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period.

### 5. Lineman league (public board)

The app has monthly and all-time league boards. Anyone using the app, including guests, can view them.

- **Only admin-verified linemen** appear. Guests and unverified members never appear.
- Shown: the admin-approved name (or "Lineman #1234"), district, league points and number of levels cleared.
- **Not shown:** email, phone, SLM ID, account ID, photo.
- **To hide your name:** Settings → Lineman league → turn off "Show my name on the board". The board then shows only "লাইনম্যান #" and the last 4 characters of your ID. To be removed from the board completely, write to [CONTACT_EMAIL].
- Monthly winner badges are for honour only. There is no money or prize.

### 6. YouTube videos

Some lessons play YouTube videos inside the app. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect device and viewing data under its own policies: [YouTube Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not give YouTube any of your account data.

### 7. Who sees your data; no sale

- **We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.**
- The app's admins (the team that runs the app) can see profiles to verify linemen and prevent abuse.
- We may disclose data if the law requires it (for example a court or government order).

### 8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account deletes your profile, progress, league results and sign-in account. Copies in security backups expire automatically within [BACKUP_DAYS] days.

### 9. Deleting your account and data

- **In the app:** Settings → "Delete account" → confirm. Deletion is immediate.
- **Without the app:** fill in the form on [ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL] ([DELETION_FORM_URL]), or email [CONTACT_EMAIL] from the Google address you signed in with, subject "Delete my SmartLineman account". We delete within 30 days.
- Guest data lives only on your phone: uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
- To delete only some data (for example your phone number), clear that field in your profile or write to us.

### 10. Not for children

The app is made for adult (18+) electricity workers and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data; if we learn of any, we delete it.

### 11. Security

All data between the phone and our servers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The database uses access rules (Row Level Security) so each member can change only their own data, and league scores are worked out only by fixed server functions. No system is 100% secure.

### 12. Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new date on this page, and tell you in the app for major changes.

### 13. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests to see, correct or delete your data, or complaints: **[OWNER_NAME]**, **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**. We reply within 30 days.
