1. আমরা কে

SmartLineman (প্যাকেজ com.smartlineman.lineman) পশ্চিমবঙ্গের বিদ্যুৎ বিতরণ লাইনম্যানদের জন্য একটি বাংলা সুরক্ষা-প্রশিক্ষণ গেম। অ্যাপটি চালান SmartLineman (ব্যক্তিগত ডেভেলপার)। যোগাযোগ: smartlinemanapp@gmail.com।

অ্যাপটি কোনো বিদ্যুৎ সংস্থা বা সরকারের অফিসিয়াল অ্যাপ নয়, এবং তাদের সঙ্গে যুক্তও নয়। একই অ্যাকাউন্ট ওয়েবসাইট smartlineman.in-এও কাজ করে; এই নীতি দুই জায়গার অ্যাকাউন্ট ডেটার জন্যই প্রযোজ্য।

English

1. Who we are

SmartLineman (package com.smartlineman.lineman) is a Bangla safety-training game for electricity distribution linemen in West Bengal, India. It is run by SmartLineman (individual developer). Contact: smartlinemanapp@gmail.com.

The app is not an official app of any electricity utility or government body and is not affiliated with them. The same account also works on the website smartlineman.in; this policy covers account data in both places.

2. What we collect and why

If you play as a Guest, no account is created. Your progress, points and settings stay only on your phone and are not sent to our servers.

If you sign in with Google, we store:

DataSourceWhy
Name, email, profile photo linkYour Google account (at sign-in)Identify your account, show your profile
Account ID (a code)Our sign-in systemLink your data to your account
Phone number (optional)You type itAdmin verification, contact if needed
District, block, job typeYou choose themVerification, district league board
SLM ID, role, verification and block statusThe app and adminsKnow who is verified, prevent abuse
Points, finished levels and lessons, level stars, time taken per level, quiz answersWhile you playSave progress, scoring, the league (the time is checked to refuse impossibly fast plays)
Game progress backup (ladder progress, stars, ◆, mistake book, toolbox)While you play, when signed inBring your progress back on a new phone or after reinstalling

We do not collect: your location (GPS), contacts, photos or files, microphone, camera, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no purchases. Read-aloud voices are made by your phone's own text-to-speech.

Some data is kept on your phone (game save, downloaded quizzes and lessons, the last league board) so the app works offline. When you are signed in, a small backup of your game progress (at most 16 KB) is kept on our server, so that after signing in on a new phone the app can ask "Bring back your earlier progress?" and restore it. Guest progress is never sent to the server. The backup is deleted with your account.

3. Google sign-in

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In. Google gives us your name, email and profile photo link. We never see your Google password. How Google handles data: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Where data is stored (Supabase)

Account and game data are stored with Supabase, a cloud database service that processes data on our behalf (data processor). Server region: Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2). Supabase's policy: supabase.com/privacy. Large images, audio and lessons download from our website smartlineman.in; like any server it may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period.

5. Lineman league (public board)

The app has monthly and all-time league boards. Anyone using the app, including guests, can view them.

  • Only admin-verified linemen appear. Guests and unverified members never appear.
  • Shown: the admin-approved name (or "Lineman #1234"), district, league points and number of levels cleared.
  • Not shown: email, phone, SLM ID, account ID, photo.
  • To hide your name: Settings → Lineman league → turn off "Show my name on the board". The board then shows only "লাইনম্যান #" and the last 4 characters of your ID. To be removed from the board completely, write to smartlinemanapp@gmail.com.
  • Monthly winner badges are for honour only. There is no money or prize.

6. YouTube videos

Some lessons play YouTube videos inside the app. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect device and viewing data under its own policies: YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy. We do not give YouTube any of your account data.

7. Who sees your data; no sale

  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The app's admins (the team that runs the app) can see profiles to verify linemen and prevent abuse.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it (for example a court or government order).

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account deletes your profile, progress, progress backup, league results (including your entries in the monthly winners list) and sign-in account. Copies in security backups expire automatically within 7 days.

9. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings → "Delete account" → confirm. Deletion is immediate. It is one shared account, so your smartlineman.in website account is deleted too.
  • Without the app: fill in the form on https://smartlinemanapp.github.io/privacy_policy/account-deletion.html (mailto:smartlinemanapp@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20SmartLineman%20account), or email smartlinemanapp@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, subject "Delete my SmartLineman account". We delete within 30 days.
  • Guest data lives only on your phone: uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
  • To delete only some data (for example your phone number), clear that field in your profile or write to us.

10. Not for children

The app is made for adult (18+) electricity workers and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data; if we learn of any, we delete it.

11. Security

All data between the phone and our servers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The database uses access rules (Row Level Security) so each member can change only their own data, and league scores are worked out only by fixed server functions. No system is 100% secure.
