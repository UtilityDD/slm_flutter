### English

```
SmartLineman is a Bangla safety-training game for electricity distribution linemen in West Bengal. Learn the real steps of field work by playing them, learn from mistakes, and get home safe every day.

The Deathless Path (মৃত্যুহীন পথ)
Home has one button. It opens a ladder of levels: first Learn, then Do it hands-on. Each module ends with a rank gate. Mistakes add to the death count and send you back to the checkpoint. Careful work earns stars.

What you learn
• PPE and dress: helmet, gloves, belt, shoes, in the right order
• Shutdown, docket and permit-to-work: what must be confirmed before work starts
• Dead test, discharge and earthing: proving the line is dead
• Pole climbing, harness, ladders and safe distances
• Tools: megger, hydraulic crimper, hot stick and more
• Transformers, fuses, insulators, jumpers, service connections
• Fault finding and line repair
• First aid, fire and storm response

How it teaches
• Fully in Bangla, with read-aloud using your phone's voice
• Field scenes, real job order, spotting a careless coworker's mistakes
• Hints only after a mistake, and the reason explained at the end
• Light enough for budget phones; downloaded lessons work offline

Tools
• Identify gear: photo library of PPE, tools, insulators and cables
• Emergency numbers, one tap to dial
• PPE checklist and the 8 safety mantras

Monthly lineman league
Admin-verified linemen earn league points by clearing levels. District, state and all-time boards. Turn off your name on the board in Settings if you prefer. The league is for honour only: no money and no prizes.

Play without an account
Start at once as a guest. Sign in with Google to save progress and join the league.

No ads. No purchases.

Important
• This is a training aid. It does not replace your employer's official training, rules, permit-to-work or your supervisor's instructions. Always follow your organisation's procedures in the field.
• SmartLineman is not an official app of any electricity utility or government body and is not affiliated with them.
• Electrical accidents and deaths are shown for training purposes.

Contact: [CONTACT_EMAIL]
```
