When settling lesson/level safety facts (deadly acts, clearances, first aid, PPE, testing), cite WBSEDCL manuals first, then Indian law/standards (CEA safety regulations, IE Rules 1956, BIS IS codes, other Indian discoms), then international (IEC, OSHA 1910.269, NFPA 70E, HSE, IFRC/Red Cross). Do not use any Bangladesh manual or law.

**Conflicts (owner, 2026-11-15):** when WBSEDCL and a national Indian source disagree (CEA, MoHFW/DGHS, BIS), follow the national source; for owner-decision items, take the most logical option without asking again.

**Why:** Owner instruction 2026-11-12 ("no bangladesh manual. ref should be wbsedcl, any indian or international ref").

**How to apply:** Every safety answer carries org + clause + quote from a source actually opened; game-rule questions (points, strikes, wording) are owner calls, not referenceable. Decision lists live in docs/decisions/ (see [[module1-owner-decisions-2026-11-08]]).
