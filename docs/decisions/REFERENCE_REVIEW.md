### A1 — Burns and water: lesson 1.7, the 1.7 board trap and the step 21 coworker say 'no water on burns', but lesson 10.17 says pour clean water for 20 min. Which is right?
- **Verdict:** confirmed · confidence high
- **Answer:** Option (a). Cool every burn, electrical burns included, with clean running water: at least 10 minutes, ideally 20. Do this only after the supply is off and the casualty is free of the conductor. The WBSEDCL Safety Manual 2025 (sec 12.2 'Electrical Burns') does not forbid water. It says nothing about cooling and goes straight to Vaseline/burn ointment, a dressing and treating shock. That does not clash with IFRC 2020, which also allows Vaseline, but only AFTER cooling. WBSEDCL 12.3 puts 'switch off power supply' first. IFRC 2020 (Burns) and Healthdirect (electrical burns) both give running water for 20 minutes. IFRC also says not to use ice-cold water or ice (risk of hypothermia), and to call EMS for any burn caused by electricity. 'No water on burns' is not supported by any source found. Lesson 10.17 is correct and lesson 1.7 is wrong.
