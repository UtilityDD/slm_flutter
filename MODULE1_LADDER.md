## How every Do level works (one game, five moves)

| Move | What the player does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Choose** | pick the right item from a dealt tray | rubber gloves, not cotton; 33 kV rod for a 33 kV docket |
| **Inspect** | close look, with a hands-on test where it fits (torch sweep, air squeeze, finger slide, tap for sound) | pinhole glove, frayed strap, hollow pole |
| **Spot** | tap the danger (or the safe place) in a real photo | tool tip inside the লক্ষ্মণ রেখা, rusted pole base, weak anchor |
| **Order** | put steps in sequence | earth → R → Y → B; P-A-S-S |
| **Say no** | a pressure moment: someone rushes you; the right answer is to stop or finish the check | "লাইন তো ৫ মিনিট বন্ধ, ডিসচার্জ লাগবে না" |

**Every mistake teaches visually:** freeze → that lesson's own **ভুল বনাম সঠিক** picture (from `slm_web/public/images/loader/`) → one line why → **পাঠে দেখুন** opens the lesson at that paragraph → saved in **আমার ভুলের খাতা** (comes back as recall).
**Deadly mistake:** 💀 + fall to the rank base (option C: only Do levels passed since the last death turn silver, "prove again"; Learn stays done). Before climbing again: read the lesson section that killed you. The first deadly mistake in the game is a near-miss (no fall).
**Same frame on every Do level:** fresh deal each play, never blocked by speech, short reactions, result card with "আজ যা ধরলেন / যা মিস করলেন", level score (latest play counts). Voice: device TTS for new levels.

## Lesson by lesson

### 1.1 পোশাক ও পিপিই (steps 1–3) — built
- **Teaches:** cotton/FR full sleeves (no polyester, no metal buttons); non-vented ISI helmet + chin strap; EH boots (no nail, no cracked sole); rubber gloves (air test every time; cotton/leather/cut gloves don't stop shock); full body harness, not waist belt; replace PPE when damaged.
- **Step 2 কবচ যাচাই** ✅ — store room; shirt/trousers/boots; flaws: grease, burn hole, torn sleeve, oil, nail, cracked sole.
- **Step 3 গ্লাভস যাচাই** ✅ — air test; pinhole, crack, wet; wrong gloves. *Proposed:* add a helmet station (torch sweep: hairline crack, vents, no chin strap).
- **Deadly:** polyester near a flash; nail in sole; pinholed/cotton gloves.
- **Pressure lines (from the myths):** "বাপ-দাদারা কোমর-রশা দিয়েই কাজ করেছে"; "একটু ফাটা হেলমেটে কিছু হয় না".
- **Mistake pictures:** `ppe11_dress`, `ppe11_helmet`, `ppe11_helmet_types`, `ppe11_shoes`, `ppe11_gloves`, `ppe11_airtest`, `ppe11_glovetype`, `ppe11_cut`, `ppe11_harness`.
- **Promise:** air-test gloves every time (saved).

### 1.2 লক্ষ্মণ রেখা (steps 4–6)
- **Teaches:** arc flash jumps without touching; minimum distances LT 1.2 m · 11 kV 2.5 m · 33 kV 3.0 m; more in rain/fog/wind/height; a long tool's tip counts as your hand; judge distance by a man's height (~5.5 ft); PVC/bamboo jugaad is not a hot stick.
- **Step 5 কতটা দূরে?** 🆕 Spot: a street with a line of known voltage (its plate on the pole); workers on a ladder, a roof, with a pole, a boom — the player drags the লক্ষ্মণ রেখা out from the wire; only its distance shows (মি · ফুট), no target and no hint. Everyone under its edge is stopped. The real band and its rule show only after the check (as the hint after a mistake). Rain variant: 11 kV grows to 10–12 ft. *(Owner approved 2026-11-08: no man-height ruler, the hint only on failure.)*
- **Step 6 লম্বা টুলের ডগা** 🆕 Choose the tool (tested fibreglass hot stick vs dry bamboo vs PVC pipe with metal hook), then drag the stick's tip (only its distance shows) to bring the kite down — or call শাটডাউন when the kite is inside. Pressure: the careless coworker peeps in, "শুকনো বাঁশ তো, চলবে".
- **Deadly:** entering the distance; bamboo/PVC near a live line.
- **Assets:** existing `bg_market_wire_down`, `pole14_*` (crowd, cones), `bamboo_ladder`, `FRP_Ladder`; mistake pictures `arc_flash`, `safe_distance`, `bamboo_vs_hot_stick`, `corona`.
- **Promise:** keep a long tool's tip outside the লক্ষ্মণ রেখা.

### 1.3 পোলের ভাষা (steps 7–9)
- **Teaches:** walk 360°, check lean and base (erosion, water, foundation crack); sound test at ground level and joints (PCC solid "ঠক্", hollow "দপ্"; steel clear "টন", dull = rust); visual scan (hairline cracks, spalling, exposed rebar; rust at ground/welds/bolts; nests, hives, cable tangle); hardware (bent cross-arm, broken insulator, loose bolts).
- **Step 8 ৩৬০° পোল পাঠ** ♻️ L11 is close: inspect a PCC pole front/back + tap for sound.
- **Step 9 স্টিলের ক্যান্সার** ♻️ L12/L13 are close: rusted base, top scan, hardware.
- **Deadly:** climbing a condemned pole.
- **Pressure:** "স্টিলের পোল শুধু বাঁকে, ভাঙে না"; "চুলের মতো ফাটল তো থাকেই".
- **Assets:** existing `pole11–13_*` photos, `pole11_hammer`; pictures `360_dgree_inspection`, `pole_sound_test`, `rusted_pole`.
- **Promise:** read the base, tap for sound, then climb.

### 1.4 হারনেস (steps 10–12)
- **Teaches:** waist belt = work positioning only, never fall arrest; 3-minute check (webbing cuts/burns/chemicals, stitching, D-rings & buckles, label expiry); fit (not twisted, 4 fingers under the straps, chest strap mid-chest, back D-ring between the shoulders); anchor only to a strong point (not a broken cross-arm or insulator pin); suspension trauma → quick rescue.
- **Step 11 তিন মিনিটের পরীক্ষা** 🆕 Choose full body vs waist belt, then inspect: finger slide along webbing (buzz at a cut), check stitching, read the label date (expired?).
- **Step 12 ফিটিং আর অ্যাঙ্কর** 🆕 Spot what's wrong on a lineman wearing the harness (twisted strap, chest strap at the neck, loose leg strap), then tap the safe anchor point on a pole photo.
- **Deadly:** waist belt for fall arrest; frayed/expired harness; weak anchor.
- **Pressure:** "২০ বছর কাজ করছি, হারনেস লাগে না"; "পুরনো হলেও মজবুত লাগছে".
- **Assets:** `Full_Body_Harness` ×3, `tray_waist_belt`, `Lanyard_Hook` ×3, `body_harness`, pose art; pictures `belt_vs_safety_belt`, `full_body_harness_1`, `harness_llose_vs_fit`, `lanyard`, `care_harness`.
- **Promise:** check webbing, stitches and hook before climbing.

### 1.5 ডিসচার্জ রড (steps 13–15)
- **Teaches:** a dead line can hold capacitive charge and induced voltage; **earth first, then line** (R, Y, B one by one; spark is good); check stick (clean, dry, no crack), copper cable, clamp/hook; rod rating must match the docket voltage; no PVC jugaad; brass/copper chain after the rod, never instead of it.
- **Step 14 সঠিক রড** 🆕 A docket says "33 kV"; choose the rod whose label matches; reject the jugaad; inspect a rod (cracked stick, cut cable, rusty clamp).
- **Step 15 আগে মাটি, পরে লাইন** 🆕 Order on a pole photo: clamp to earth → touch R → Y → B → throw the chain; at the end remove in reverse. Pressure: "লাইন তো ৫ মিনিট বন্ধ, ডিসচার্জ লাগবে না".
- **Deadly:** line before earth; wrong rating; jugaad; chain instead of rod; skipping discharge.
- **Assets:** `discharge_rod` ×2, `Copper_Shorting_Chain`, `Portable_Earth_Rod`, `gear_discharge_rod`, `prop_earth_set`; pictures `parts_of_discharge_rod`, `wrong_vs_right_discharge_rod`, `daily_check_discharge_rod`, `discharge_jugaad_vs_standard`, `chain15_rod_plus_chain`, `chain15_dos_donts`.
- **Promise:** earth first, line last — never the other way.

### 1.6 পারমিট-টু-ওয়ার্ক (steps 16–18)
- **Teaches:** PTW = written permit for a named person, place, time, job; golden steps (request → isolation + LOTO → permit number → receive, then discharge & test); line clear in reverse (everyone down, tools off, call, cancel, LOTO off); match the feeder name on the PTW to the site; a phone "line is off" is not a permit; permits expire.
- **Step 17 কাগজ মেলাও** 🆕 Spot: a PTW form beside a pole's feeder tag — find the mismatch (feeder name, date/time expired, no signature). Then the phone rings: "লাইন বন্ধ আছে, শুরু করো" — say no without paper.
- **Step 18 সোনালি ধাপ ও লাইন ক্লিয়ার** 🆕 Order the permit steps, then the line-clear steps (count the team!).
- **Deadly:** working on a verbal permit / the wrong feeder; skipping the test after the permit.
- **Assets:** `gear_ptw`, `LOTO_Kit`, `gear_lock_board`, `prop_lock_board`, `bg_control_room`, `operator_control`; pictures `ptw_form_key_fields`, `ptw_golden_steps_flow`, `line_clear_workflow`, `verbal_vs_written_ptw`, `loto_lock_tag_breaker`.
- **Promise:** no written permit in hand, no hand on the line.

### 1.7 শক লাগলে ফার্স্ট এইড (steps 19–21)
- **Teaches:** never touch the victim; switch off the source (switch/MCB/call control room) or separate with a dry non-conductor (dry wood, bamboo, plastic chair, hot stick) — push the limbs, not the chest/head; check response; send someone to call the ambulance; CPR if trained; no mud/burying; LT shocks also kill.
- **Step 20 ছোঁবেন না!** 🆕 Timed: a colleague is stuck to a live wire. Choose the first action (call to switch off / touch / pull by the shirt), pick the object (dry bamboo vs wet stick vs iron rod vs plastic chair), tap where to push.
- **Step 21 গোল্ডেন মিনিট** 🆕 Order: check response → call → CPR; a bystander shouts "মাটিতে পুঁতে দাও!" — say no.
- **Deadly:** touching the victim; using a wet/metal object.
- **Assets (new art needed for the scene):** pictures `shock_do_not_touch`, `shock_disconnect_source`, `shock_check_response`, `shock_cpr_steps`, `shock_myth_mud_vs_ambulance`.
- **Promise:** never touch a shocked person bare-handed — power off first.

### 1.8 মাঠে আগুন (steps 22–24)
- **Teaches:** Class A (grass, wood, paper), B (transformer oil, diesel), C (live electrical); never water on B or C; power off first when safe (C may then become A/B); DCP (ABC), CO₂ (B/C), sand (small oil fire); gauge in the green; PASS; wind at your back; keep the escape route open; call the fire brigade when it's beyond you.
- **Step 23 কোন আগুনে কী** 🆕 Three fires (burning transformer oil, sparking panel, dry grass under a pole): choose the right extinguisher for each; one bucket of water is the trap; inspect the gauge before use. Lottie scene (2026-11-07): live flames and smoke, hold to spray at the base, buckets thrown, rescue bloom, flare-up on water.
- **Step 24 PASS** 🆕 Order P-A-S-S, aim at the base (spot), and choose where to stand (wind behind, exit behind you).
- **Deadly:** water on an electrical or oil fire.
- **Assets:** `Fire_ABC_Extinguisher`; pictures `fire_class_abc`, `fire_no_water_bc`, `fire_extinguisher_types`, `fire_pass_method`, `fire_myth_refill_required`. A fire scene needs new art.
- **Promise:** never water on an electrical fire — power off first.

### 1.9 ঝড়-বৃষ্টিতে 'না' (steps 25–27)
- **Teaches:** rain = slippery pole, conductive air/PPE, poor visibility; lightning = red line, no PPE helps — stop and come down; strong wind = balance, dust, conductor galloping; saying no to unsafe work is your right and duty; plan a safe shelter.
- **Step 26 আকাশ পড়ুন** 🆕 Say no: a job in progress while the weather turns (drizzle → wind, galloping wires → first thunder); at each cue choose continue / stop; the right moment matters.
- **Step 27 সুপারভাইজারের চাপ** 🆕 "হালকা বৃষ্টি, কাজটা শেষ করো" — say no; choose the safe shelter (not under the pole or a lone tree). ♻️ L9/L10 (storm night, branch on line) meanwhile.
- **Deadly:** staying on the pole in lightning; climbing in a storm under pressure.
- **Assets:** `bg_pole_storm`, `bg_tree_on_line`; pictures `storm_rain_hazards`, `storm_lightning_stop`, `storm_wind_galloping`, `storm_say_no_stop_work`, `storm_myth_ppe_lightning`, `extra_precaution_during_rain`.
- **Promise:** thunder means come down.

### 1.10 ১০ সেকেন্ডের মেন্টাল চেকলিস্ট (steps 28–30)
- **Teaches:** a pilot-style 10-second scan before the feet leave the ground — personal (PPE, harness, tools, permit, my state of mind) and surroundings (pole checked, লক্ষ্মণ রেখা, other hazards: water/traffic/bees, weather, escape & rescue plan); stop on any doubt; run it with your mate.
- **Step 29 ১০ সেকেন্ডের স্ক্যান** 🆕 Ten quick cards in a row, each a photo with one question ("হারনেসের ফিতে?", "পোল দেখেছেন?", "আকাশ?"), 2–3 seconds each: go or stop. Each card links to its lesson — a mistake opens that lesson's picture. The whole module recalled in under a minute.
- **Step 30 র‍্যাঙ্ক গেট: প্রথম ডকেট** ♻️ L33 field night now. Target: one full job — kit from the store → permit check → pole check → distance → discharge order → a weather call — every move, every deadly rule of the module. Pass = **ট্রেইনি**; a clean module (0 deaths) = **মৃত্যুহীন** mark and a fresh helmet.
- **Pictures:** `10s_check_list`, `choose_right_path`.
